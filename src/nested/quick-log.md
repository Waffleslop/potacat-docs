# Quick Log

**Ctrl+L** opens a log dialog for contacts that aren't in the spot table — useful for logging a QSO you found by tuning around.

When you log an unspotted station, you can classify the contact as POTA, DX, or other — POTACAT then re-spots the station on that network.

> **Tip:** **Ctrl+L** doubles as a previous-QSO lookup: type any callsign into the dialog to see your prior contacts with that station.
