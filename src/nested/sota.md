# SOTA

Summits on the Air spots from the SOTA API. Orange markers on the map positioned at summit coordinates.

**Hunting an activator who is on a park and a summit:** when the activator was spotted on both POTA and SOTA, the contact is logged under the park with the summit alongside. From 1.14.2 the SOTA chase is uploaded to SOTAdata as well. If SOTA upload is on but no SOTAdata login is saved, the log says so.
