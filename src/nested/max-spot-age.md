# Max Spot Age

**Max Spot Age (minutes):** is in **Settings > Spots**. Spots older than this are hidden. The minimum is 1 and the default is 5.

- It applies to WWFF, LLOTA and WWBOTA spots. POTA spots stay until pota.app expires them, unless you tick **Apply the max spot age to POTA too**.
- **SOTA Max Spot Age (minutes):** is separate: minimum 5, default 30.
- **DX Cluster Max Spot Age (minutes):** is separate: minimum 1, default 15.
- RBN has its own age box in the Propagation view, 30 by default.
