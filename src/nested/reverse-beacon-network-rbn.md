# Reverse Beacon Network (RBN)

The RBN shows where your CQ calls (and watchlist callsigns) are being heard by the worldwide skimmer network.

**Setup:** there is nothing to enable. RBN connects whenever your callsign is set.

Its view is **More ▾ > Propagation**, which shows RBN and PSKReporter together; in an activation, the **Prop** button opens it. It shows a map with band-colored circle markers (sized by SNR), a spot table, and a dynamic legend. Filters for band and max age are at the top; the max age starts at 30 minutes.
