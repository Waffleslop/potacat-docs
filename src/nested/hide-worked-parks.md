# Hide Worked Parks

**Hide worked parks** is in the **Spots ▾** dropdown. It hides POTA spots for parks you have already hunted. It applies to POTA spots only, and parks you have activated don't count.

The same setting is **Hide already-worked parks** in **Settings > Spots**.

The list of hunted parks comes from your POTA hunter CSV and from the parks in your POTACAT log, so it works without a CSV too. See [Parks Worked](parks-worked.md).
