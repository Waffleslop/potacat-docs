# DX Expedition Tracking

POTACAT checks Club Log for active DX expeditions. Matched spots receive:
- A **DXP** source badge
- **Pinned-to-top** positioning in the table
- A **red/gold map marker**

Active events are shown in the Spots dropdown → Events & Awards section with a track/progress button.

## The event banner

An event you can take part in shows a banner. From 1.14.3 you can switch a banner off, which helps with an event that runs all year.

Click the event's badge on the banner (the "250" of America250 WAS, for example). It opens:

- **Show this banner**
- **Track my progress**
- **Stations on my watchlist**
- **Open the board**

A banner you switch off stays off, whether you track the event or not. To bring it back, tick its **Banner** box in **Settings > Logging > Events & Awards**, or in the Spots menu under **Events & Awards**.
