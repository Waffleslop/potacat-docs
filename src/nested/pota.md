# POTA

Parks on the Air spots from the [POTA API](https://api.pota.app). Refreshed automatically: every 30 seconds by default. **Refresh Interval (seconds)** in Settings takes 15 to 300. Green markers on the map.

**Parks Worked:** Import your hunted-parks CSV from pota.app in **Settings > Spots**, under **Spot Sources**: **POTA Parks Worked CSV:** > **Browse**. Parks in your POTACAT log count too. Spots for parks you've already worked show no "NEW" badge, and you can filter them out with **Hide worked parks** in the **Spots ▾** dropdown. See [Parks Worked](parks-worked.md).

**Stats overlay:** When parks data is loaded, click the Stats button in the status bar to see your Parks Worked count, Total QSOs, unique locations, and how many new-to-you parks are on the air right now.
