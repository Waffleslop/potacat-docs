# Pop-out QSO Logbook

Press **F2** or click the **Logbook** button in the toolbar to open a detachable logbook window showing all your logged QSOs.

Features:
- **Search** across all fields
- **Sortable columns** (click headers)
- **Inline edit** — click any cell to modify
- **Delete** — two-click delete (click once to confirm, click again to remove)
- **Export ADIF** — save your entire log
- **Real-time updates** — new QSOs appear instantly via toast notifications
- **Stats footer** — total QSO count and session summary

From 1.14.6:

- **Rig column.** Every new contact stores the name of the rig it was logged on, from [My Rigs](my-rigs.md), in the ADIF `MY_RIG` field. The **Rig** column is off by default, and you can edit it.
- **Source column.** A contact logged from ECHOCAT or ECHOCAT Web is marked as such. The **Source** column is off by default.
- **JS8 and JTTY** contacts show as JS8 and JTTY in the Logbook, counts, filters, worked-before and the DXCC tracker, instead of MFSK.
- Every logged and forwarded contact carries the DXCC entity number, so N3FJP and other loggers place the country correctly.
