# Settings Reference

Open Settings from the toolbar dropdown → "Open Settings..." or from the welcome screen.

## Display

- **Change Theme:** from 1.14.0, one **Change Theme** dropdown, in the Settings sidebar, Settings > Display and the Welcome screen, lists every look, including **Miami Nights** and **She's On Frequency** (1.13.10). It replaces the light/dark switches.
- **Waterfall colours:** from 1.14.0, the ECHOCAT app's waterfall palettes are on every desktop waterfall. Pick one in Settings > Display, the FT8 window's Options or the Band Scope. **Standard** keeps each window's own colours.

## Station Setup and Report a Bug

At the bottom of the Settings tab list on the left:

- **Station Setup** is a checklist for your radio: what is working and what still needs doing. It's also in the **More ▾** menu. From 1.14.0 it has a **Voice transmit** step after the data transmit test. If its test transmit can't go out where the radio is tuned, it moves to a clear spot in the band's data segment, at least 3.5 kHz from the FT8, FT4, FT2, JS8 and WSPR frequencies, transmits there and tunes back; the log says where. If nothing on that band is in your privileges, it says so and doesn't key.
- **🐞 Report a Bug** records a detailed log while you reproduce a problem. See [Report a Bug](community--support.md#report-a-bug).
